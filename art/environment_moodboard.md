# Environment Moodboards - "The Office"

This document outlines the visual direction for the different zones in the project. All designs follow a **2:1 Isometric Pixel Art** style with vibrant, high-contrast colors and cel-shading.

---

## 🟦 OFFICE (Main Workspace)

The core of the simulation. A balance between productivity and modern aesthetics.

![OFFICE Moodboard](./office_moodboard.png)

### Design Elements:
- **Color Scheme**: Primary Blue (#3b82f6), Slate, and Cool Greys.
- **Furniture**: White minimalist desks, dark ergonomic chairs.
- **Props**: Multi-monitor setups, small potted succulents, cable grommets.
- **Lighting**: Bright, top-left dominant light creating clean shadows.

---

## 🟪 FOCUS_ROOM (Concentration Zone)

A minimalist 4x4 space designed for deep focus and zero distractions.

### Design Guidelines:
- **Color Scheme**: Deep Purple, Violet accents, and Charcoal.
- **Wall Treatment**: Sound-absorbing hexagonal foam panels in shades of purple.
- **Furniture**: A single high-tech standing desk.
- **Atmosphere**: Low ambient light with a single focused task lamp (warm glow).
- **Interactive**: Entering this zone could trigger a "Do Not Disturb" status in Discord.

---

## 🟧 CAFETERIA (Social Hub)

The warm and inviting area for breaks and casual conversation.

### Design Guidelines:
- **Color Scheme**: Vibrant Orange (#f97316), Warm Wood tones, and Cream.
- **Furniture**: Round café tables, stools, and a long counter.
- **Props**: Espresso machine (interactive), snack vending machines, mugs left on tables.
- **Lighting**: Warm, diffuse lighting to create a cozy "golden hour" vibe.

---

## 🟪 ARCADE (Minigame Zone)

A playful, retro-futuristic space for community interaction.

### Design Guidelines:
- **Color Scheme**: Neon Pink (#ec4899), Electric Blue, and Deep Black.
- **Atmosphere**: Dark room with glowing neon accents.
- **Props**: Retro arcade cabinets, a pinball machine, and a neon "THE OFFICE" sign.
- **Floor**: Checkered or dark carpet with glowing patterns.
- **Interactive**: Arcade machines should be "usable" triggers for Discord minigames.

---

## 🟦 STUDIO (Creative / Presentation)

The hub for teamwork, brainstorming, and formal presentations.

### Design Guidelines:
- **Color Scheme**: Cyan (#06b6d4), White, and Light Grey.
- **Wall Treatment**: Large interactive whiteboards and glass partitions.
- **Furniture**: Modular tables that can be rearranged, a large presentation screen.
- **Props**: Projector hanging from the ceiling, markers, sticky notes on walls.
- **Lighting**: Flat, even professional studio lighting.

---

## Technical Specifications (Reminder)
- **Perspective**: Isometric 2:1 (26.565° angle).
- **Tile Size**: 32x32 pixels.
- **Lighting**: Consistent top-left source across all rooms.
- **Shadows**: Hard-edged (no gradients), following the 2:1 ratio.
